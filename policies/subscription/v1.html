On the phone it is activated on, Pro opens: the English letters after E and e, English numbers, the tracing themes (bought with the coins the child earns), the colouring pictures marked with a crown, all the bubble-pop letters and numbers, adding your own examples to any letter, numbers 10 to 100, and the Noorani Qaida.   4. 4. We send you an activation code. Paste it into the app; Pro opens at once, without the internet.

4. No automatic renewal

A code ends on its last day, judged by the phone's own calendar, at the end of that day. Nothing is ever charged again by itself. The app reminds you 7, 3 and 1 days before the end; to go on, ask us for a new code. When Pro ends, the child's progress stays and the free parts keep working. Tracing themes already bought stay the child's.

5. One phone

A code works only on the phone it was made for. A code for another phone, whether new, replaced or reset, is a new subscription: we cannot check that the first phone no longer uses its code. Resetting a phone to its factory settings gives it a new device code, so it needs a new subscription too. Setting the phone's clock back does not extend a code.
